# The o32 OpenGL driver (`IP32CRM`): routines whose code differs from the N32 build

The O2 ships the OpenGL driver `libGLcore.so` twice: an N32 build under `usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM32/`, covered by [libglcore.md](libglcore.md) and [opengl.md](opengl.md), and an o32 build under `usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/`, whose routines are compiled differently. A routine is understood only in the build whose code was read, so the o32 build needs its own entries wherever its code differs from the N32 code of the same name; routines with identical code share one entry. This page holds the o32 entries. Each was written from the Ghidra decompilation of the whole routine in the [o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so) (6.5.30), checked against the register stores of the inventory scan.

Facts every entry relies on, read from the code of this build: the GL context keeps two views of the rendering engine's register window, at +0x7db8 and +0x7dbc, and the drivers write each register through both; draw-mode and primitive words are cached in the context (+0x7d58 with its copy at +0x7d5c, primitive +0x7d74 with its copy at +0x7d60) so that an unchanged word is not stored again. Register offsets are those of [CRIME_RE.h](../CRIME_RE.h). The inventory scan lists register sites for some routines that only call a method through the context; those entries say that no register is written.

## Small state and flush routines

- `__glCrmGenericPickStoreProcs` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): chooses the span-store method of the context, 220 bytes at offset 0xd970c20. - `__glCrmInitStencil` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): initialises the stencil buffer record of a context, 224 bytes at offset 0xd970634. After the generic buffer initialiser it stores the stencil depth in bits from +0xcb0 of the context at +0xc of the record, the bytes per pixel (depth plus the word at +0xcac, plus 7, divided by 8) at +0x18, zeroes +0x10, +0x14 and +0x40, and fills the record's method slots (+0x2c make-current, +0x34 and +0x38 the same routine, +0x58 to +0x64 and +0x74 the driver's routines, +0x68 to +0x70 no-ops). It writes no engine register. Established.
- `__glCrmPassDepthFunc` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): passes the depth test and its range to the engine, 420 bytes at offset 0xd947fa8. It builds a depth word with the comparison (low three bits of the word at +0x660) in bits 1 to 3 and a 24-bit depth scale made from the double at +0x668 times the unsigned integer at +0x7a48 shifted right by 6. When the byte at +0x7a5c is zero and bit 2 of the draw-mode word at +0x7d58 is set, it sets bit 24 of the depth word and bit 0 of the byte at +0x7d5b, stores a stencil mode word (a fixed pattern with the byte from +0x7a4c in the top) to `+0x21e0` and stores `+0x21e8` as all ones in the low byte or as zero by bit 1 of the draw-mode word; otherwise it clears bit 24 and, if the word at +0x7a60 is set, bit 0 of the byte. The depth word goes to `+0x21c0` of both mappings. Established.
- `__glcrmim_RenderMode` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): the driver's glRenderMode entry, 312 bytes at offset 0xd963ca4. It then calls the generic render-mode routine and returns its result. It writes no engine register. Established.

- `__glCrmFogCILine` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): draws a fogged colour-index line, 356 bytes at offset 0xd94adf0. - `__glCrmPassColorMask` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): passes the colour write mask, 484 bytes at offset 0xd93f13c, read as far as its two main branches. ## Routines the decompiler cannot read

The o32 build uses the MIPS IV reciprocal and conditional-move forms (`recip.s`, `movz.s`) that the decompiler does not decode, so these entries were written from the disassembly (`objdump -m mips:isa64`) of the whole routine.

- `__glCrmSmoothCILine` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): draws a Gouraud-shaded colour-index line, 268 bytes at offset 0xd936a28. It takes the two end points' x and y floats, picks the longer axis by comparing the absolute differences (clamping a zero difference with a constant from the context) and takes its reciprocal. The two vertices' 64-bit words go to `+0x2080` and `+0x2088` of the first mapping; the primitive word at +0x7d74 goes to `+0x2060` of both mappings when it differs from its cached copy. When bit 8 of the enable word at +0x700 (line stipple) is set, the first time since the flag byte at +0x7498 was cleared it stores the stipple mode and pattern words from +0x7d6c and +0x7d70 to `+0x20c0` and `+0x20c4` of both mappings and sets the flag, and an x-major line with the stipple enabled whose length is 127 pixels or more leaves through the shared tail of the depth-line routine. It then takes the two colour indices (first float of each colour record), scales the first by the float at +0x7d10 and the difference over the longer axis by the same factor, and stores them as integers: the start value to `+0x20ec` and the slope to `+0x2104` (x-major) or `+0x210c` (y-major). A final zero store through the start alias (`+0x2904` or `+0x290c`) starts the line. Established.
