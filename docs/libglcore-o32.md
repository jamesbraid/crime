# The o32 OpenGL driver (`IP32CRM`): routines whose code differs from the N32 build

The O2 ships the OpenGL driver `libGLcore.so` twice: an N32 build under `usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM32/`, covered by [libglcore.md](libglcore.md) and [opengl.md](opengl.md), and an o32 build under `usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/`, whose routines are compiled differently. A routine is understood only in the build whose code was read, so the o32 build needs its own entries wherever its code differs from the N32 code of the same name; routines with identical code share one entry. This page holds the o32 entries. Each was written from the Ghidra decompilation of the whole routine in the [o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so) (6.5.30), checked against the register stores of the inventory scan.

Facts every entry relies on, read from the code of this build: the GL context keeps two views of the rendering engine's register window, at +0x7db8 and +0x7dbc, and the drivers write each register through both; draw-mode and primitive words are cached in the context (+0x7d58 with its copy at +0x7d5c, primitive +0x7d74 with its copy at +0x7d60) so that an unchanged word is not stored again. Register offsets are those of [CRIME_RE.h](../CRIME_RE.h). The inventory scan lists register sites for some routines that only call a method through the context; those entries say that no register is written.

## Small state and flush routines

- `__glCrmFlushAndConfirm` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): stores zero to the flush register `+0x21f8` of the mapping at +0x7db8 and then reads the status word at `+0x4000` in a loop until bit 28 of it reads set, 48 bytes at offset 0xd943124. Established.
- `__glCrmColorOptLine` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): the colour-only line entry, 52 bytes at offset 0xd936720. When the context's primitive word (+0x7d74) differs from its cached copy it stores the word to the primitive register `+0x2060` of both mappings and refreshes the cache. It then stores the first vertex's 64-bit word (at +0x80 of the vertex record) to `+0x2080` and the second vertex's to `+0x2888`, the start-alias form of `+0x2088`, which starts the line. Established.
- `__glBeginLLoop` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): prepares the context for a loop over vertex lists, 56 bytes at offset 0xd937814. It clears the byte at +0x7498, points the word at +0x3190 at +0x31a8 of the context and installs three method pointers at +0x2eac, +0x2f24 and +0x2ef4 (the matrix-validate and loop-end hooks). It writes no engine register. Established.
- `__glCrmAllocAccum` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): forwards to the context's method at +0x7ab8 with its argument and the words at +0xe74 and at +0xe78 (68 bytes at offset 0xd941c68); it writes no engine register. Established.
- `__glCrmPassStencilMask` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): when the byte at +0x7d09 is set and bit 15 of the enable word at +0x700 (stencil test) is set, it stores the sign-extended halfword at +0x68a (the GL stencil write mask) to the stencil mask register `+0x21e8` of both mappings, 72 bytes at offset 0xd96f710. Established.
- `__glCrmPolygonOffsetPoint` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): renders a point with the polygon offset applied, 92 bytes at offset 0xd95c0a4. Established.
- `__glCrmBeginPolygon` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): starts a polygon, 104 bytes at offset 0xd9640ac. - `__glCrmEnableDepthTest` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): recomputes bit 2 of the byte at +0x7d5b (the depth-test bit of the draw mode) from the mode byte at +0x7d08 and bit 4 of the enable word at +0x700, then calls the depth-mask pass routine, 108 bytes at offset 0xd94814c. - `__glCrmEnableDither` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): copies the dither enable (bit 3 of +0x700) into bit 0 of the byte at +0x7d5a, then, unless the word at fixed address 0x1018 equals 2 and only when the draw-mode word at +0x7d58 differs from its cached copy at +0x7d5c, stores it to draw mode `+0x2018` of both mappings and updates the cache, 120 bytes at offset 0xd93ece8. - `__glCrmEnableLogicOp` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): when the byte at +0xc94 is zero it copies the logic-op enable (bit 2 of +0x700) into bit 1 of the byte at +0x7d5a and updates draw mode `+0x2018` of both mappings from the word at +0x7d58 as the dither routine does, 132 bytes at offset 0xd93ed60. Established.
- `__glCrmPassAlphaFunc` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): passes the alpha test to the engine, 160 bytes at offset 0xd93e654. It scales the reference float at +0x720 by 255, converts it to an integer (a value of 2^31 or more is first wrapped), places it in the low byte of a word with the four-bit comparison code from +0x71c in the next byte, and stores the word to `+0x2198` of both mappings. Established.
- `__glCrmPassFog` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): unless the byte at +0xc95 is set, it passes the OpenGL fog colour to the engine, 244 bytes at offset 0xd93f048. The three colour floats at +0x638, +0x63c and +0x640 (already scaled to 0 to 255) are converted to integers, a value of 2^31 or more being wrapped first, and packed with the first in the top byte, the second in the next and the third in the byte below that; the word goes to fog colour `+0x2170` of both mappings. - `__glCrmPassBlendColor` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): passes the constant blend colour, 340 bytes at offset 0xd93eb94. It multiplies the four colour floats at +0x730, +0x734, +0x738 and +0x73c by a double constant held in the data section, wraps a result at or above 2^31, truncates each to an integer and packs them with the float at +0x730 in the top byte and the one at +0x73c in the low byte; the word goes to `+0x21a0` of both mappings. Established.
- `__glCrmSetDrawBuffer` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): selects the buffer that drawing goes to, 384 bytes at offset 0xd9428a4. It asks a context helper to resolve the OpenGL buffer name into a buffer-mode word, stores the word to destination buffer mode `+0x2008` of both mappings and keeps it at +0x80cc. If the resolved name differs from the one cached for that buffer slot, it maps the name (front or front-and-back to 0, back to 1, the depth enum to 2) into a request record with a draw flag, the slot number and the drawable's value, sends ioctl `0x55f7` to the kernel through the driver's ioctl wrapper and reports a failure through the context's error method at +0x14. Established.
- `__glCrmSetReadBuffer` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): the read-buffer twin of the draw-buffer routine, 384 bytes at offset 0xd942a24. It resolves the read buffer name, stores the word to source buffer mode `+0x2000` of both mappings, keeps it at +0x80d0, and on a change sends the same ioctl `0x55f7` with the read flag set and the read drawable from +0x7d3c. Established.
- `__glCrmGenericPickStoreProcs` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): chooses the span-store method of the context, 220 bytes at offset 0xd970c20. From the draw buffer name at +0x760 and the dither and blend bits (mask 0x24) of the word at +0x76e4 it stores one of four method addresses in the driver, or the method held in the record at +0x2df0, into +0x3140, and for the single-buffer path also +0x3144. It writes no engine register: the texture registers `+0x2110` to `+0x2168` that the inventory scan lists for it are not stored by this routine. Established.
- `__glCrmInitStencil` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): initialises the stencil buffer record of a context, 224 bytes at offset 0xd970634. After the generic buffer initialiser it stores the stencil depth in bits from +0xcb0 of the context at +0xc of the record, the bytes per pixel (depth plus the word at +0xcac, plus 7, divided by 8) at +0x18, zeroes +0x10, +0x14 and +0x40, and fills the record's method slots (+0x2c make-current, +0x34 and +0x38 the same routine, +0x58 to +0x64 and +0x74 the driver's routines, +0x68 to +0x70 no-ops). It writes no engine register. Established.
- `__glCrmPassDepthFunc` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): passes the depth test and its range to the engine, 420 bytes at offset 0xd947fa8. It builds a depth word with the comparison (low three bits of the word at +0x660) in bits 1 to 3 and a 24-bit depth scale made from the double at +0x668 times the unsigned integer at +0x7a48 shifted right by 6. When the byte at +0x7a5c is zero and bit 2 of the draw-mode word at +0x7d58 is set, it sets bit 24 of the depth word and bit 0 of the byte at +0x7d5b, stores a stencil mode word (a fixed pattern with the byte from +0x7a4c in the top) to `+0x21e0` and stores `+0x21e8` as all ones in the low byte or as zero by bit 1 of the draw-mode word; otherwise it clears bit 24 and, if the word at +0x7a60 is set, bit 0 of the byte. The depth word goes to `+0x21c0` of both mappings. Established.
- `__glcrmim_RenderMode` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): the driver's glRenderMode entry, 312 bytes at offset 0xd963ca4. Inside a begin and end pair (the word at fixed address 0x1018 equals 1) it raises error 0x502; a mode name it does not recognise raises 0x500. Switching into the render mode installs the driver's raster-position methods at +0x3148, +0x314c and +0x3150 and subtracts half a pixel from the raster position floats at +0x6bc and +0x6c4; leaving it restores the generic methods and adds the half pixel back. It then calls the generic render-mode routine and returns its result. It writes no engine register. Established.
- `__glCrmFlatTriangle` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): draws one flat-shaded triangle, 236 bytes at offset 0xd934c40. It chooses a primitive word by the sign of the word at +0x75e4 and the caller's last argument (`0x2010000` or `0x2020000` when the sign is negative, `0x2030000` or `0x2000000` otherwise) and stores it to `+0x2060` of the first mapping only. The three vertices' 64-bit words (at +0x80 of each record) go to `+0x2080`, `+0x2088` and `+0x2090`. When bit 1 of the word at +0x807c is set it commits with a zero store to `+0x29f0`, the start-alias form of `+0x21f0`; otherwise it packs the colour floats of the current colour record into one word and stores it through the start alias `+0x28d0` of the foreground colour `+0x20d0`, which starts the triangle. Established.
- `__glCrmFillStippledTriangle` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): fills a triangle under the polygon stipple, 236 bytes at offset 0xd945c0c. It saves the draw-mode word (+0x7d58) and the enable word (+0x76e4), sets bit 0 of the byte at +0x7d5b and clears bit 4 of the enable word (the stipple), stores the new draw mode to `+0x2018` of both mappings when it differs from the cached copy, calls the generic triangle method at +0x2f34, then restores the saved enable and draw-mode words and stores the draw mode again if it changed. Established.
- `__glCrmFogCILine` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): draws a fogged colour-index line, 356 bytes at offset 0xd94adf0. It runs the fog method at +0x30f4 for each end point with the point's colour record (+8) and fog value (+0xb0), substituting temporary colour records, then (unless bit 16 of the saved draw-mode word is set) sets bit 0 of the byte at +0x7d59 and stores the draw mode to `+0x2018` of both mappings if it changed. It calls the smooth colour-index line routine, clears the same bit and stores the draw mode again if it changed, and puts the original colour records back. Established.
- `__glCrmRenderPointRGBA` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): draws one RGBA point, 136 bytes at offset 0xd95d1b0. It stores the vertex's 64-bit word (at +0x80 of the record) to `+0x2080` of the first mapping and clears the word at +0x8080. When the primitive word at +0x7d64 differs from its cached copy at +0x7d60 it stores it to `+0x2060` of both mappings and refreshes the cache. It finally packs the four colour floats of the vertex's colour record (the fourth in the low byte, the first in the top byte) and stores the word through the start alias `+0x28d0` of the foreground colour, which starts the point. Established.
- `__glCrmFillFrontBackTriangle` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): fills a triangle in two buffers in turn, 512 bytes at offset 0xd945cf8. It saves the draw-mode word and the stencil mask of the second mapping, selects the first store buffer (id at +0x77c4) through a driver helper, clears bit 1 of the byte at +0x7d5b, stores draw mode `+0x2018` if it changed and sets the stencil mask `+0x21e8` to zero in both mappings, and calls the triangle method at +0x8104. It then selects the second store buffer (id at +0x77c0), restores bit 1 from the saved draw mode, stores `+0x2018` again if needed, restores the stencil mask and calls the triangle method a second time. Inferred: the last few instructions that restore the saved draw mode were not read.
- `__glCrmPassColorMask` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): passes the colour write mask, 484 bytes at offset 0xd93f13c, read as far as its two main branches. With a zero draw-buffer name it does nothing. With the byte at +0xc94 zero (colour-index mode) it builds the mask from the word at +0x758 limited to the index bit count at +0xcb4 and records it at +0x80c0. Inferred: the final stores were not followed.

## Routines the decompiler cannot read

The o32 build uses the MIPS IV reciprocal and conditional-move forms (`recip.s`, `movz.s`) that the decompiler does not decode, so these entries were written from the disassembly (`objdump -m mips:isa64`) of the whole routine.

- `__glCrmSmoothCILine` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): draws a Gouraud-shaded colour-index line, 268 bytes at offset 0xd936a28. It takes the two end points' x and y floats, picks the longer axis by comparing the absolute differences (clamping a zero difference with a constant from the context) and takes its reciprocal. The two vertices' 64-bit words go to `+0x2080` and `+0x2088` of the first mapping; the primitive word at +0x7d74 goes to `+0x2060` of both mappings when it differs from its cached copy. When bit 8 of the enable word at +0x700 (line stipple) is set, the first time since the flag byte at +0x7498 was cleared it stores the stipple mode and pattern words from +0x7d6c and +0x7d70 to `+0x20c0` and `+0x20c4` of both mappings and sets the flag, and an x-major line with the stipple enabled whose length is 127 pixels or more leaves through the shared tail of the depth-line routine. It then takes the two colour indices (first float of each colour record), scales the first by the float at +0x7d10 and the difference over the longer axis by the same factor, and stores them as integers: the start value to `+0x20ec` and the slope to `+0x2104` (x-major) or `+0x210c` (y-major). A final zero store through the start alias (`+0x2904` or `+0x290c`) starts the line. Established.
- `__glCrmSmoothRGBLine` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): draws a Gouraud-shaded RGBA line, 388 bytes at offset 0xd9368a4. It finds the longer axis from the absolute x and y differences, returns at once for a zero length and takes the reciprocal of the longer difference. It stores the two vertex words to `+0x2080` and `+0x2088`, the primitive word to `+0x2060` of both mappings when it changed, and the stipple mode and pattern to `+0x20c0` and `+0x20c4` under the same bit-8 stipple test as the colour-index line, with the same 127-pixel exit for an x-major stippled line. The start colour is the four floats of the first vertex's colour record plus a rounding constant from +0x7d14, whose raw bits are stored: red and green as one 64-bit store to `+0x20e0`, then `+0x20e8` and `+0x20ec`. Each slope is the colour difference times the reciprocal and a scale at +0x7d10, truncated to an integer. An x-major line stores the slopes to `+0x20f0`, `+0x20f4` and `+0x2100`, clears the y slopes (`+0x20f8` to `+0x20fc`, `+0x2108` and `+0x210c`) and ends with the alpha slope through the start alias `+0x2904`, which starts the line; a y-major line does the mirror image with `+0x20f8`, `+0x20fc`, `+0x2108` and the alias `+0x290c`. Established.
- `__glCrmFlatZTriangle` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): draws a flat-coloured, depth-tested triangle, 412 bytes at offset 0xd934d2c. It takes the reciprocal of the triangle's signed area (the float at +0x75e0, with the sign word at +0x75e4), chooses the primitive word by that sign and the caller's last argument (`0x2010000`, `0x2020000`, `0x2030000` or `0x2000000`) and the vertex that supplies the flat colour, and stores the word to `+0x2060` of the first mapping and the three vertices' 64-bit words to `+0x2080`, `+0x2088` and `+0x2090`. It then forms the depth plane from the three vertices' depth floats (at +0x88) and the screen-space edge deltas: the two slopes go as 64-bit integers to `+0x21d0` and `+0x21d8`, and the start depth to `+0x21c8` as a 64-bit integer. The routine ends through a start alias: the packed colour word to `+0x28d0` (or one colour index when the enable word's bit 0 is clear), or, when bit 1 of the byte at +0x808c says colour is not written, the start depth through `+0x29c8`. Established.
- `__glCrmSmoothCIZTriangle` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): draws a Gouraud-shaded colour-index triangle with depth, 428 bytes at offset 0xd935458. It reciprocates the signed area (+0x75e0), ORs one of four direction words (`0x0`, `0x10000`, `0x20000`, `0x30000`, by the sign word at +0x75e4 and the caller's flag) into the primitive base at +0x7d68 and stores it to `+0x2060` of the first mapping; the three vertex words go to `+0x2080`, `+0x2088` and `+0x2090`. - `__glCrmSmoothRGBTriangle` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): draws a Gouraud-shaded RGB triangle without depth or alpha, 508 bytes at offset 0xd935004. - `__glCrmFogRGBLine` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): draws a fogged RGBA line, 652 bytes at offset 0xd936b34. ## Colour, point and vertex routines

- `__glCrmRenderPointDepthRGBA` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): draws one depth-tested RGBA point, 168 bytes at offset 0xd95d238. It ends with the vertex depth float (record +0x88) times the scale at +0x7d18, converted to an integer and stored as a 64-bit word through the start alias `+0x29c8`, which starts the point. Established.
- `__glcrmim_Indexf` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): the driver's glIndexf entry, 188 bytes at offset 0xd941238. When the colour-valid word at +0x8080 is zero or the value differs from the cached one at +0x8084 it stores the value to foreground colour `+0x20d0` of both mappings and updates the cache. It always leaves +0x8080 at 2. Established.
- `__glCrmDownloadCurrentColor` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): sends the current colour to the engine, 212 bytes at offset 0xd940ff0. In colour-index mode (byte +0xc94 zero) the value is the index float at +0x1c0 masked by +0x7810; otherwise it is the four floats at +0xc0, +0xc4, +0xc8 and +0xcc packed with the first in the top byte and the fourth in the low byte. - `__glCrmVertexPointFast` in `libGLcore.so` ([o32 build](../root/usr/gfx/arch/IP32CRM/libGLcore.so)): emits one point vertex, 212 bytes at offset 0xd95af38. Nothing happens when the vertex's flag word at +4 is nonzero. Established.
