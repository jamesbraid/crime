# SGI frame-buffer layer in the X module (`nfb`)

`usr/lib/X11/dyDDX/crm.so` carries 165 routines whose names begin `nfb`. They are the graphics vendor's own layer between the machine-independent server code and the CRIME drawing routines of [xmodule.md](xmodule.md). No public source holds the layer as a unit. A search of the public document and source indexes for the names found only disassembly published in this repository. Where a routine is a modified copy of a public sample-server routine, the entry names the public file in the X.Org server tree at tag `XORG-6_8_2` (`mi`, `cfb` and `mfb`) and says what the vendor changed; the others are described from the body alone.

Each entry was written after reading the whole body of the routine in the 6.5.30 module (`objdump -d`, calls resolved through the module's global offset table, canonical `$gp` `0x5b0ea0`). **Established** means the whole body was read and every call, store and branch the entry states was seen; **Inferred** means the role rests on a part of the body or on a sibling.

Conventions used in the entries, all read from the module:

- The screen record keeps its drawing-method slots at the same displacements as the public screen record with these extra anchors established elsewhere in this repository: close at +0x98, window creation at +0xb4, window destruction at +0xb8, background paint at +0xd8, pixmap destruction at +0xf0, cursor display at +0x11c, colormap creation at +0x134, region destruction at +0x15c, device-private array at +0x1b4. The nfb screen record is the word at +0x74.
- A graphics context keeps its screen at +0, its private array at +0x4c and the clientClip fields at +0x10 (type in bits 18 and 19) and +0x38. The nfb private record of a context is found through the module global `nfbGCPrivateIndex`, and a window's through `nfbWindowPrivateIndex`.
- A table at +0x20 of the nfb context private holds the drawing methods in the order of the public context-operations table, one word each: slot +0x0c copy-area, +0x10 copy-plane, +0x34 and +0x38 the two poly-text methods, +0x3c and +0x40 the two image-text methods, +0x4c push-pixels.

## Stereo wrappers

The `nfbStereo` routines wrap one drawing method of the context's operations table to draw a window twice, once for each eye of a stereo frame buffer. Every uniform member does the same bookkeeping, read in each body: it saves the context's operations pointer (+0x48) and swaps in the table held at +0x5c of the nfb private, sets the re-entry flag at +0x68, then twice picks one of two composite clips (the words at +0x60 and +0x64, stored at +8), moves the drawable's y halfword (+0xa) and the private's clip-origin y halfword (+0x5a) by the offset at +0xe2 of the nfb screen record (down for one eye, up for the other, the order chosen by whether the word at +0xdc of that record is 1), and calls the method; at the end it puts back the saved clip, operations pointer and flag. The entries below give what differs.

- `nfbStereoPolyPoint` in `crm.so`: draws the points for each eye through slot +0x14 (the point method) of the context's operations, handing over its five arguments unchanged, 448 bytes at offset 0x55a720. Established.

## Raster-operation loops

The module holds one unmasked and one masked word loop for each of the sixteen X logical operations, numbered as the protocol numbers them (0 clear, 3 copy, 5 leave unchanged, 6 exclusive or, 15 set). Every unmasked loop takes a source pointer, a destination pointer and a destination end pointer and combines 32-bit words from the destination start up to the end; every masked loop takes a fourth argument, a word mask, and writes the combined bits only where the mask is set, keeping the destination bits elsewhere. Each body is unrolled by four or eight words with a short head loop for the remainder and prefetch instructions ahead of the pointers. The loops touch memory only.

- `nfb0RopLoop..EG` in `crm.so`: the unmasked loop of operation 0: it clears every destination word to zero; 280 bytes at offset 0x560490. Established.
- `nfb0MaskRopLoop..EH` in `crm.so`: the masked loop of operation 0: it clears every destination word to zero, but only in the bits the mask selects, leaving the other destination bits as they were; 1460 bytes at offset 0x57b390. Established.
- `nfb1RopLoop..FG` in `crm.so`: the unmasked loop of operation 1: it replaces each destination word with the bitwise and of the source and destination words; 184 bytes at offset 0x5718c0. Established.
- `nfb1MaskRopLoop..FH` in `crm.so`: the masked loop of operation 1: it replaces each destination word with the bitwise and of the source and destination words, but only in the bits the mask selects, leaving the other destination bits as they were; 248 bytes at offset 0x5981a0. Established.
- `nfb2RopLoop..GG` in `crm.so`: the unmasked loop of operation 2: it replaces each destination word with the source word and the complement of the destination word; 200 bytes at offset 0x57ec00. Established.
- `nfb2MaskRopLoop..GH` in `crm.so`: the masked loop of operation 2: it replaces each destination word with the source word and the complement of the destination word, but only in the bits the mask selects, leaving the other destination bits as they were; 264 bytes at offset 0x57b270. Established.
- `nfb3RopLoop..HG` in `crm.so`: the unmasked loop of operation 3: it copies each source word over the destination word; 184 bytes at offset 0x58a620. Established.
- `nfb3MaskRopLoop..HH` in `crm.so`: the masked loop of operation 3: it copies each source word over the destination word, but only in the bits the mask selects, leaving the other destination bits as they were; 232 bytes at offset 0x5986f0. Established.
- `nfb4RopLoop..IG` in `crm.so`: the unmasked loop of operation 4: it replaces each destination word with the complement of the source word and the destination word; 200 bytes at offset 0x5672a0. Established.

## Including-inferiors wrappers

Twelve `nfbInf` routines stand in the graphics-context operations table for a context whose drawing is allowed to cover child windows. Each one, read in its body, tests a module-wide re-entry flag (the global at `$gp-26096`) and the bit for the drawable's depth in the mask at +0x18 of the nfb private. When the flag is clear and the bit is set it sets the flag, saves its arguments in module globals, clears bit 17 of the context's word at +0x10, hands the real operation to `nfbInfRender..IJ` through the method from the table at +0x20 of the nfb private, restores the bit and the window's private pointer, clears the flag, and, if a second module global was raised meanwhile, copies the context's foreground, background, plane mask and logical operation into the nfb private and runs its method at +0x1c. In every other case it calls the same method directly. The entries give each request's method slot.
